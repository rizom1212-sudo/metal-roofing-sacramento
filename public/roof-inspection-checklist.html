Pre-Storm Roof Checklist

PRC 13 Roofing Inc. · Sacramento, CA · Lic. No. 1087153 · 916-761-3866

From the ground (safe, no ladder required)

  • Missing, cracked, or curled shingles visible from the street
  • Granules collecting in gutters or on the ground
  • Sagging ridge lines or dips in the roof plane
  • Damaged or detached gutters and downspouts
  • Flashing pulled away at chimneys, vents, or skylights
  • Tree branches touching or overhanging the roof

Inside the home

  • Water stains or discoloration on ceilings or walls
  • Peeling paint near roof lines or in upstairs rooms
  • Musty odor in attic or upper floors after rain
  • Daylight visible through attic decking boards
  • Wet insulation in the attic after storms

After a storm

  • Document damage with photos (roof, gutters, interior stains)
  • Place buckets under active leaks; move valuables away from wet areas
  • Do not climb on the roof. Call a licensed roofer for assessment
  • Contact your insurer if damage may be storm-related
